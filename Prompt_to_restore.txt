Revise the Referral Letter and Supporting Summary in the Student Well-Being Early Warning, Intervention & Referral Support System.
The objective is to produce a professional Malaysian government school referral letter that is supportive, objective, non-judgemental, and suitable for referral to hospitals, Klinik Kesihatan, Clinical Psychologists or Child Psychiatrists.
The referral letter must not imply that the student has a psychiatric disorder or mental illness.
General Requirements
Follow Malaysian Ministry of Education (KPM) official letter writing style.
The referral letter should:

* sound professional and respectful
* use neutral and objective language
* describe school observations only
* avoid making medical conclusions
* emphasise collaboration between school, parents and healthcare professionals
* request professional assessment instead of suggesting diagnosis
* keep the letter concise (approximately one page)

The letter should be signed by either:

* Guru Besar
or
* Penolong Kanan Hal Ehwal Murid

Referral Letter
Replace strong statements
Avoid words or phrases such as:

* menunjukkan tanda-tanda tekanan emosi yang membimbangkan
* simptom masih berterusan
* penambahbaikan tidak tercapai sepenuhnya
* masalah mental
* gangguan mental
* rawatan psikiatri
* psychiatric case
* severe emotional problems
* gagal menunjukkan perubahan
* kes yang serius

Instead use neutral wording such as:

* berdasarkan pemerhatian pihak sekolah
* pihak sekolah telah membuat beberapa pemerhatian
* perkembangan murid masih memerlukan perhatian
* pihak sekolah telah melaksanakan intervensi awal
* pihak sekolah berpendapat penilaian lanjut dapat membantu memahami keperluan murid
* bagi mendapatkan pandangan profesional
* bagi membantu pihak sekolah menyediakan sokongan yang bersesuaian

The purpose of the referral should be:

* mendapatkan penilaian profesional
